About

ADU Tour Guide is your all-in-one uni app. It brings together a GPA calculator, grade and GPA targets, a schedule generator, a degree planner and a group project splitter, so you can plan your semester and your degree in one place.

It’s free, needs no sign-up, and your data stays in your browser. Export a CSV any time to back it up or move it to another device.
